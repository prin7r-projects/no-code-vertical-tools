## Slide 4 — Anatomy of a tool (TT-HVAC-002)

Six pieces, every time. Only the names of the parts change between verticals.

1. Input form (vertical-specific fields)
2. Pricing rules (your real numbers)
3. Branded artifact (PDF / SMS / email — your domain, your logo)
4. Pay link (Stripe or USDT/USDC)
5. Notifier (SMS / WhatsApp / Slack / calendar)
6. Tuning notes (1-page monthly)

> "Same six pieces in every tool. Only the names of the parts change."

1. **Catalog beats platform for the trade buyer.** She knows the workflow she wants fixed. She doesn't want to discover possibilities.
2. **Vertical-specific shipping is faster than generalist no-code.** A pre-built dental no-show predictor beats a Bubble-from-scratch every time.
3. **The price clears procurement-free decision.** $39 lives below the corporate-card-without-a-meeting line in every trade we sell to.
4. **Crypto-first lowers our take rate vs Stripe** by 1.8% — passed to the buyer as a lower price + higher gross margin to us.
